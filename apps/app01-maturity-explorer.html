What the labels mean

Overall verdict. Taken as a whole, AI in deformation monitoring is in early adoption: the methods work on real data and some practitioners use them, mainly to screen data and give earlier notice of trends, but they are not yet standard practice. Deep learning, and warnings issued by AI without a person reviewing them, are still experimental. Monitoring codes set trigger levels on measured values and do not yet provide for AI to replace those checks.
