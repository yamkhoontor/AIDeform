App 3.3: Congruence Test

Chapter 3 · Established

Five points of a control network are surveyed in two epochs. Move one or two of them, set the precision of the survey, and see whether the global congruence test finds that the network has deformed, and which points are responsible. The sample calculation of Section 3.4 is the same test applied to one point at a time.

Settings

Point by point

What to notice. The settings start with the sample calculation of Section 3.4: point A moves 6 mm and point B 1.5 mm, with a standard deviation of 1.5 mm per coordinate. A gives a test value of 4.0, above 3.00, and has moved; B gives 0.25 and has not. The global test asks one question of the whole network, has it kept its shape? With only one of five points moved it is diluted by the stable ones; increase A's movement and it fails too, and localisation then removes the largest movement first until the rest pass. Raise the standard deviation to 3 mm and the same 6 mm is no longer significant: a movement is always judged against the precision of the survey. Tick "add survey noise" to see how random errors blur the picture.
