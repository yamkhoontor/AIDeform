App 6: Thermal Cycle and Detectors

Chapter 6 · Experimental

The tunnel ring of App 5 now swings with a daily thermal cycle. Fifteen problems are planted in the test period (days 31 to 60). Compare an Isolation Forest that sees only the displacements, the same detector given air temperature and hour of day, and a small autoencoder trained in your browser. Thresholds are set at the 99.5th percentile of scores on days 26 to 30.

Settings

Press Run after changing settings. The autoencoder is trained in your browser and takes a few seconds.

Events found and false alarms

Training…

What to notice. Without a temperature input, the detector flags the midday peak of the thermal swing as unusual. Giving it temperature and hour of day cuts the false alarms, often by as much as a more complex model does. Good inputs often matter more than a complex model. The autoencoder here is a small teaching version of the LSTM autoencoder in Chapter 6; deep learning in monitoring remains experimental.
