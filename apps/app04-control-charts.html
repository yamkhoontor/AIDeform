App 4: Control Charts

Chapter 4 · Established

Twelve settlement points behind a retaining wall are read daily for 120 days; excavation starts on day 61. Tune the CUSUM and EWMA charts and the rate rule, and see how many days of notice they give before the Alert Level is reached, and how many false flags they raise on points that are not moving.

Settings

All twelve points

Signals are counted from day 61. "Notice" is the number of days between the CUSUM signal and the Alert Level. Points SP01, SP06 and SP08 to SP12 do not move, so any signal there is a false alarm. The baseline screen flags readings with a modified z-score above the limit.

What to notice. A smaller CUSUM decision line or EWMA weight signals earlier but raises more false alarms on the quiet points. With the book's settings (k = 0.5, h = 5, λ = 0.1) SP03 signals about a month before its Alert Level, but over 60 days one or two quiet points may still raise a false flag. Change the random seed to see how this varies, and raise h to trade notice for fewer false alarms.
