App 5: Ring Detectors

Chapter 5 · Early adoption

A simulated tunnel ring of eight prisms is read every 30 minutes for 60 days, giving 24 readings per epoch (radial, tangential and longitudinal). After a clean baseline, you plant three kinds of problem: single-prism faults, slow drifts of one prism, and ring-wide shape changes. Six detectors are trained on the baseline and scored on everything that follows.

Settings

Changes are applied when you press Run, because training six detectors takes a few seconds.

Events found and false alarms

Training detectors…

An event counts as found if any reading inside it is flagged. False alarms are flags outside the planted events, after the baseline, per 1,000 readings. Thresholds are set from the baseline so that about the chosen share of baseline readings would be flagged; the Mahalanobis distance uses its χ² limit.

What to notice. No single method wins. Per-prism tests catch faults but raise many false alarms, because they run 24 tests at every epoch. Group methods see ring-shape changes that no single prism shows. Isolation Forest, popular elsewhere, struggles to isolate one wrong reading among 24, which is why every AI method needs a classical benchmark.
