App 2: Signal and Noise

Chapter 2 · Established

Every monitoring reading mixes three things: real movement, a daily thermal cycle and random noise. Set each one and see how easily the real movement can be seen. The second panel works out the smallest change you can detect between two readings.

Settings

Smallest detectable change between two readings

For two readings each with standard deviation σ, the difference has standard deviation √2 σ. At 95 % confidence the smallest detectable change is about 2.8 σ.

What to notice. Set the thermal swing larger than the daily settlement and the real movement disappears in the raw readings, yet a straight line fitted through many readings still finds the rate. That is why trends and statistics beat reading-by-reading comparison.
