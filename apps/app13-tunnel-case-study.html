App 13: Tunnel Case Study

Chapter 13 · Established Early adoption

Replay the 120-day project of Chapter 13: a 14 m deep basement beside a railway tunnel, with fifteen monitored rings (R40 to R54), five prisms each. Move the trigger levels and the agreed warning probability, decide whether the engineer acts on an early warning, and see when each escalation stage would have been entered and what happens to ring R47. Data are daily means and illustrative.

Settings

Project log

What to notice. Untick "the engineer acts on Stage 2" and the extra strut is never installed: R47 carries on past its Alert Level as the excavation deepens. The early warning matters only if someone acts on it. Lower the warning probability and Stage 2 comes earlier; raise it and you rely more on the steady-approach rule.
