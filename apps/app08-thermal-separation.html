App 8: Thermal Separation

Chapter 8 · Established Early adoption

A building corner beside a cut-and-cover tunnel is read every 30 minutes for 90 days. Its readings swing every day with the sun and settle faster while the excavation passes. Split the record into trend, daily cycle and what is left; check that the cycle is thermal; and let change-point detection find the days when the rate of settlement changed.

Settings

Change points against the construction log

What to notice. With the thermal part removed, change-point detection finds the stages of the excavation within a few days. The delay between air temperature and the cycle shows the structure warming up. Raise the penalty and it finds fewer changes; lower it and it starts chasing noise. A change with no matching site event deserves investigation.
