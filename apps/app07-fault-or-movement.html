App 7: Fault or Movement?

Chapter 7 · Established Early adoption

Eleven tunnel wall prisms, TW10 to TW20, are read every 30 minutes from two total stations. Choose what happens at 02:30 and the app works through the diagnostic questions of Chapter 7, then shows what a random-forest classifier, trained on simulated past flags, suggests as the cause.
