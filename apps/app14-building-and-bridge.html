App 14: Building and Bridge

Chapter 14 · Established Early adoption

Two short case studies from Chapter 14. A 40-storey tower leans with the sun every day while a slow lean develops beneath it; a bridge deck expands and contracts with temperature while a bearing sticks and then slips. Change the size of the thermal swing and of the real movement, and compare an alarm set on the raw readings with one set on the residual after the temperature effect is removed.

Settings

Raw readings or residuals?

Both alarms use a limit of four standard deviations, set from the first 30 days. A false alarm is a reading over the limit before the real movement began. A thermal model is fitted to the first 30 days by least squares on air temperature delayed by the chosen time.

What to notice. On the raw readings the limit has to be wide enough to hold the daily swing, so the real movement must grow large before it is seen. On the residuals the same real movement stands out within days. Make the thermal swing larger and the raw alarm gets worse still, while the residual alarm hardly changes.
