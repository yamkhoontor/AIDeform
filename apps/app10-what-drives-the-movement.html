App 10: What Drives the Movement?

Chapter 10 · Established Early adoption

An inclinometer in a diaphragm wall reports the maximum deflection every hour for 100 days. You set how strongly each cause really acts: excavation depth, groundwater drawdown, temperature and rainfall. Then see whether a regression with significance tests, and a gradient boosting model questioned by permutation importance and partial dependence, can recover what you set.

Each effect is estimated by least squares with its standard deviation and tested at 95 %. VIF (variance inflation factor) above about 10 means the cause moves together with another one, so their separate effects cannot be told apart. Depth and depth below 8 m are linked by design, so their VIFs are raised, but with this much data both are still well determined.

Permutation importance

How much worse the gradient boosting model gets when one input is shuffled, as a share of the total.

What to notice. Tick "use both standpipes" and the two groundwater readings move together: their VIFs soar and their separate effects become meaningless. Set rainfall to zero and both methods rank it last. When the regression and the machine learning model agree, the conclusion is stronger; correlation is still only a lead, to be checked against the construction record.
