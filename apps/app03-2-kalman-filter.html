App 3.2: Kalman Filter

Chapter 3 · Established

A settlement point is read once a day for 60 days. A Kalman filter follows it reading by reading, estimating where the point is and how fast it is moving, flagging readings that surprise it, and forecasting ahead with an uncertainty band. Change the movement, the noise and the filter's agility, and follow the calculation step by step.

Settings

Step by step: the last six days

Each day the filter predicts the reading from yesterday's estimate and velocity, compares the prediction with the reading (the innovation), and moves part of the way towards the reading. The share it moves is the gain. The surprise is the innovation divided by its expected size; beyond ±2.58 the reading is flagged (99 %). This is the calculation of Section 3.3, here with the velocity estimated too.

What to notice. Raise the agility and the filter follows changes faster but becomes noisier; lower it and the estimate is smooth but slow to react. A sudden jump gives a large surprise on the day it happens. Untick "treat a flagged reading as a step" and the filter takes days to catch up, flagging reading after reading; in practice a confirmed step is accepted and the filter restarted from it. Start an acceleration and watch the velocity estimate climb, and the forecast band widen with the forecast horizon. Move "today" back to see what the filter would have said earlier.
