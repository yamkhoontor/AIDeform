App 3: Classical Toolkit

Chapter 3 · Established

Three classical tools are the benchmarks for every AI method in the book. Add gross errors and compare least squares with a robust Huber fit; insert a jump and watch the Kalman filter's surprise value; move a network point and run the congruence test.

1. Trend fitting: least squares and Huber

2. Kalman filter: following a point and measuring surprise

3. Congruence test: has the network kept its shape?

What to notice. A few gross errors drag the least squares line; the Huber fit stays close to the true rate. The Kalman filter's surprise jumps at a sudden step. In the congruence test, a movement smaller than about three times the reading precision is hard to detect, exactly as Chapter 2 predicts.
