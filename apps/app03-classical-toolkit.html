App 3.1: Least Squares and Robust Fitting

Chapter 3 · Established

Least squares is the backbone of deformation analysis and the first benchmark every AI method must beat. Fit a straight line to 40 daily readings, see the rate with its standard deviation and t-test, then add gross errors and compare least squares with a robust Huber fit.

Trend fitting: least squares and Huber

What to notice. With no gross errors, least squares gives the rate and its standard deviation, and the t-test says whether the movement is real (Section 3.1). Add a few gross errors and the least squares line is dragged away; the Huber fit gives them less and less weight and stays close to the true rate (Section 3.2). Make the true rate small and the noise large, and the t-test can no longer tell the movement from noise.
