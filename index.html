AI for Deformation Monitoring: Companion Apps

Anomaly Detection, Trend Analysis and Early Warning for Surveyors and Engineers · Yam Khoon Tor

These fourteen apps go with the “Try it” boxes in the book. Each one runs a small simulated monitoring problem in your browser: move a slider and the charts and results change at once. Nothing is sent anywhere and nothing needs installing.

How mature are these methods? Each app carries the book’s labels. Established methods can be used routinely. Early adoption methods work on real data but should run alongside an established method. Experimental methods are for study and trials only. Taken as a whole, AI in deformation monitoring is at the early adoption stage; deep learning, and warnings issued by AI without a person reviewing them, are still experimental.

Using the apps offline

  1. On the GitHub page of this repository, press Code, then Download ZIP.
  2. Unzip the folder anywhere on your computer.
  3. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. Every app then works with no internet connection.

The apps work on a phone or tablet too, though some of the charts are easier to read on a larger screen.
